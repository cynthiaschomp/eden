**Eden** puts everything you run in one place: your sites, servers, tickets, calendar and agents, shown as a live 3D deck you can talk to. An AI assistant, which you name yourself, watches it with you, answers questions from real data, and asks before it acts.

> **Status: early and in active development. Nothing here is installable yet.**

---

## What Exists Today

* **God's Eye**, in prototype: a live 3D view of everything you run, fed by a hub that gathers real data, rearranging itself around what you are discussing.
* **An assistant engine** with a written code of conduct, an approval step before any action, and an audit log.
* **A conduct test suite** that an AI model must pass before it is allowed to act.

---

## What Is Coming

1. **God Mode.** Eden takes the whole screen the moment you log in. One key flips between Eden and your apps.
2. **Eden as your desktop.** The bar and the launcher become Eden's, on Linux, with your assistant one keystroke away.
3. **A local model built in.** The assistant runs on your own machine, offline, with nothing leaving it. Larger models on your own server or in the cloud are optional.
4. **Eden OS.** An installer image that boots straight into Eden.

---

## Principles

* **You approve actions.** The assistant proposes; nothing runs without your consent.
* **Your data stays yours.** Local first.
* **Honest by design.** The assistant says what it is, says when it does not know, and refuses work that would deceive people.
